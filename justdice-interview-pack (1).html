Before You Apply

A few things to know

We believe in being upfront. Here's what matters most going into this process — read it honestly.

🤖
Daily AI coding fluency is non-negotiable
This role requires daily, hands-on fluency with AI coding tools — tools like Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor. Not occasional use. Not "I've tried it." We're talking about a workflow where AI is your co-pilot every single day. If that doesn't describe you right now, this isn't the right moment to apply — and that's completely okay.
⚙️
Full stack experience is required
You need meaningful hands-on experience with React Native on the frontend and backend development experience — familiarity with Go is a strong advantage. We're not looking for someone who can figure it out over time. If you're genuinely full stack with real shipped products, you're in the right place.
🤝
We value authenticity over polish
Throughout the process, we're not looking for rehearsed answers or impressive-sounding stories. We want to understand how you actually think and work. Be honest about what you know and what you don't. Be specific about what you've built and how you built it. The candidates who do best here are the ones who show up as themselves — curious, direct, and genuinely excited about the problem.
🧠
Product thinking and curiosity are required
This role isn't just about writing code — it's about caring deeply about what gets built and why. We need someone who asks questions before jumping to solutions, who sees features from the user's perspective, and who is genuinely curious about the product space. If engineering and product feel like two separate worlds to you, this isn't the right fit.

Good To Know

Quick answers

How long does the whole process take?
+
We aim to move in 2–3 weeks from intro call to offer. We'll be transparent about timelines at every stage and you'll always know where you stand.
Do you offer visa sponsorship?
+
Yes. We actively support relocation and visa sponsorship. Our team spans 20+ nationalities and we're experienced at making it work smoothly.
Why 2–4 years specifically?
+
We want someone still in growth mode — hungry, adaptable, and building something to prove. Candidates with 6–7+ years often fit a different kind of role and team. This isn't about capability, it's about the type of challenge that excites you.
What does success look like in the first 90 days?
+
Visible, accelerating productivity. Shipping new features at a pace that improves on our current baseline. By month three, you should feel deeply embedded in the product and confident making decisions independently — without being asked.
Will I get feedback if I'm unsuccessful?
+
Yes. We treat candidates with respect. You'll get specific, honest feedback — not a generic response.
